How monthly debts work

Enter the full monthly installment. We set aside monthly ×12/26 for pay every 14 days, ×12/52 for weekly pay, or a share based on actual calendar days for exact-date plans. Allocation begins with the planning start so an earlier paycheck can fund an upcoming payment, and stops after the final month. Paid skips allocation in the first installment’s month. Use “Debt payment (this period)” for a full amount you must fund now, and enter each debt once.

ONE GOOD MONEY HABIT

Save for the bill before it’s a bill.

A sinking fund is money set aside for a known future expense. If an annual bill is 6,000 and you have 6 paydays left, reserving 1,000 each payday spreads the cost. Keep that reserve separate from your everyday spending.
